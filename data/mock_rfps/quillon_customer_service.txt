I. INTRODUCTION

A. About Quillon. Quillon Outdoor Supply sells camping, climbing and paddling gear online and through 34 stores in the United States. The company handles about 60,000 customer contacts a month about orders, returns, warranties and product questions.

B. Current System. Quillon has used Zendesk Suite for customer service since 2019. The Zendesk contract ends on March 31, 2027, and Quillon is evaluating replacements that combine customer service with a single customer record across sales and marketing.

C. Users. The platform will be used by approximately 120 customer service agents and team leads.

D. Budget. Quillon has budgeted $140,000 per year for subscription fees, and up to $45,000 one time for implementation and data migration.

E. Contract Term. Quillon prefers a two-year initial term.

II. RFP SCHEDULE

A. RFP issued: November 3, 2026.

B. Questions due by email: November 13, 2026.

C. Proposals due: December 1, 2026 at 5:00 PM Mountain Time.

D. Vendor demonstrations: December 14 to 18, 2026.

E. Target go-live: March 15, 2027.

III. REQUIREMENTS

A. Case Management

1. Agents must manage customer contacts as cases with priorities, service level targets and automatic escalation.

2. The platform must keep one customer record showing orders, cases, emails and marketing activity together.

3. The platform must detect duplicate customer records and let admins merge them.

4. Agents must be able to attach files, such as photos of damaged products, to cases.

B. Channels

1. Customers must be able to contact Quillon by email, web form and live chat, with every conversation captured as a case.

2. The platform must support phone support. Quillon's agents take about 15,000 calls a month and need calls logged on cases automatically, either natively or through an integration with a contact center such as Twilio.

C. Self-Service

1. The platform must include a public help center and an internal knowledge base, with article versions and approvals.

D. Artificial Intelligence

1. The platform should summarize long cases and suggest knowledge articles and reply drafts to agents.

2. Quillon's customer data must not be used to train any vendor or third-party AI model.

E. Integrations

1. The platform must integrate with Oracle NetSuite for orders, returns and refunds.

2. The platform should integrate with Shopify Plus, which runs Quillon's online store.

3. The vendor must provide a REST API and bulk data import and export.

4. The platform must support single sign-on with Okta and enforce multi-factor authentication.

F. Reporting

1. Team leads must have real-time dashboards for queue sizes, response times and customer satisfaction scores, and a report builder for custom reports.

IV. SECURITY, PRIVACY AND HOSTING

A. The vendor must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate.

B. The vendor must comply with GDPR and CCPA and sign a data processing agreement.

C. EU data residency for records of customers in the European Union is preferred but not required.

D. The vendor must guarantee at least 99.9% monthly uptime.

E. Data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

V. IMPLEMENTATION AND SUPPORT

A. The platform must go live by March 15, 2027, before the Zendesk contract ends.

B. The vendor must migrate about 2.4 million historical tickets and 900,000 customer records from Zendesk.

C. The vendor must train team leads and agents, including recorded training for new hires.

D. Support must be available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for critical issues.

E. Quillon expects a named customer success manager.

VI. PRICING

A. Vendors must quote per-agent pricing for 120 agents for each of two years, plus any platform fees.

B. Vendors must quote implementation and migration as a fixed fee.

C. Vendors should state any multi-year discount and any cap on renewal price increases.

VII. PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

A. Proposals must be emailed as a single PDF by the due date.

B. Proposals must answer every requirement in Sections III to V with Yes, Partial or No and a short explanation.

C. Proposals must include two references from retail or e-commerce customers.

VIII. EVALUATION CRITERIA

A. Fit to requirements: 40 points.

B. Total cost over two years: 25 points.

C. Implementation plan and migration approach: 20 points.

D. References: 15 points.
