You will receive the opening pages of a request for proposal (RFP). Return:

- **buyer**: the organization issuing the RFP, for example "City of Tulsa". Use the organization's name only, never a person.
- **title**: the RFP's title, including its number if one is given, for example "RFP 26-901 Constituent Relationship Management Software".
- **due_date**: the date and time proposals are due, as an ISO 8601 date-time (YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM). Use 17:00 if only a date is given. Use null if no due date is stated.

Copy names and numbers exactly as written. Do not include contact names, email addresses or phone numbers.
